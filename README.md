Westar Energy was the largest electric utility in Kansas, providing electric service to homes, businesses, and industries throughout Kansas before merging with Great Plains Energy in 2018 to form Evergy, Inc. As Evergy, the company continues to serve approximately 1.6 million customers in Kansas and Missouri with a focus on grid modernization, smart metering (AMI) through its Gridstream Connect IoT platform, and clean energy transformation including wind power and demand response programs.

## Features

| Name | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Smart Metering (AMI) | Advanced Metering Infrastructure with Gridstream Connect IoT platform providing near real-time electricity usage data, outage detection, and remote meter reading for 1.6 million customers. |
| Energy Usage Data Access | Customer-facing energy usage analytics and comparison tools enabling visibility into historical consumption, billing trends, and efficiency benchmarking. |
| Time-of-Use Rates | Smart meter-enabled time-of-use and demand-based rate programs providing price signals and automated demand response capabilities. |
| Green Button Data | Standardized NAESB ESPI energy usage data download capability allowing customers and third parties to access electricity consumption data in Green Button format. |
| Grid Modernization | Distribution automation, substation digitization, high-voltage transmission upgrades, and distribution system modernization enabling smart grid operations and clean energy integration. |
| Rooftop Solar Integration | Net metering programs and interconnection services for distributed energy resources including rooftop solar and battery storage systems. |
